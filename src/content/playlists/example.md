I started making this playlist as a place for songs that could hold a whole evening together without asking for too much attention. The first half feels like being outside after everyone else has gone home; the second half is what happens once you finally get inside.

It is less about a specific genre than a specific temperature. I wanted the songs to feel slightly worn around the edges, like something you've listened to enough times to remember the shape of it without remembering exactly when you first heard it.
