---
title: "Night Bus"
year: 2026
description: "A short story about the strange intimacy of getting home after midnight."
---

The bus came sometime after midnight.

There were only four of us on it, each sitting in a different corner as if the seats had been assigned according to some private rule.

Outside, the city had emptied itself into reflections. Every storefront looked closed. Every apartment window looked like a small decision someone else had already made.

I kept the window cold against my forehead and watched the lights move across the glass.

For a while, I thought about getting off early.

I didn't.
